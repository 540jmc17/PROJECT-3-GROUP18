What is CampusGear?

CampusGear is a website being developed for KU students and staff to make borrowing university equipment easier. Students will be able to find items such as laptops, cameras, calculators, microphones, and other equipment used for classes and projects.

For Students

Students will be able to browse available equipment, choose reservation dates, and keep track of their reservations and borrowed items. They will also be able to view their borrowing history and return deadlines.

For Staff

Staff members will be able to add and update equipment, manage reservations, and check items in and out. They will also be able to report damaged equipment and manage maintenance requests.

Managing Equipment

Each item will have a status showing whether it is available, reserved, checked out, or under maintenance. A staff dashboard will provide information about equipment availability, current checkouts, and overdue returns.

Our Goal: give students and staff one organized place to manage shared university equipment.
